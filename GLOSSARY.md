# Glossary - terms, acronyms & entities

76 entries spanning OSINT disciplines, EW/spectrum, UAS/C-UAS, PNT, sensors, tracking, medical (TCCC), security, doctrine, and the key data **entities** this tool ingests.


### C2

- **C2** — Command and Control: Exercise of authority over assigned forces.
- **C4ISR** — Command, Control, Communications, Computers, ISR: Integrated C2 + ISR enterprise.
- **ISR** — Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance: Sensing + collection feeding decisions.
- **ISTAR** — Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition, Reconnaissance: ISR plus target acquisition.

### cyber

- **ATT&CK** — Adversarial Tactics, Techniques & Common Knowledge: MITRE knowledge base of adversary behavior.
- **IOC** — Indicator of Compromise: Artifact indicating malicious activity.
- **TTP** — Tactics, Techniques, Procedures: How an actor operates; behavior over indicators.

### discipline

- **COMINT** — Communications Intelligence: SIGINT from communications content/metadata.
- **ELINT** — Electronic Intelligence: SIGINT from non-communication emitters (e.g. radar).
- **FININT** — Financial Intelligence: Intelligence from financial flows/sanctions data.
- **GEOINT** — Geospatial Intelligence: Imagery + geospatial analysis of activity on the earth.
- **HUMINT** — Human Intelligence: Intelligence from human sources.
- **IMINT** — Imagery Intelligence: Intelligence from imagery (satellite, aerial, FMV).
- **MASINT** — Measurement and Signature Intelligence: Technical signatures (acoustic, seismic, RF).
- **OSINT** — Open-Source Intelligence: Intelligence from publicly available sources.
- **SIGINT** — Signals Intelligence: Intelligence from intercepted signals (COMINT + ELINT).
- **SOCMINT** — Social Media Intelligence: OSINT from social-media platforms.
- **TECHINT** — Technical Intelligence: Intelligence on adversary materiel/capabilities.

### doctrine

- **COA** — Course of Action: A candidate plan considered during planning.
- **EEFI** — Essential Elements of Friendly Information: Friendly info to protect from the adversary.
- **IHL** — International Humanitarian Law: Law of armed conflict protecting non-combatants.
- **IPB** — Intelligence Preparation of the Battlefield: Systematic analysis of terrain/weather/threat.
- **PIR** — Priority Intelligence Requirement: Commander's prioritized intelligence questions.
- **ROE** — Rules of Engagement: Directives on when/how force may be used.

### entity

- **ACLED** — Armed Conflict Location & Event Data: Leading disaggregated conflict-event dataset.
- **AMTI** — Asia Maritime Transparency Initiative: CSIS South China Sea monitoring.
- **CSIS** — Center for Strategic & International Studies: US think-tank; runs AMTI.
- **GDELT** — Global Database of Events, Language & Tone: Open machine-coded global event stream.
- **GTD** — Global Terrorism Database: START's open database of terrorist attacks.
- **ISW** — Institute for the Study of War: Daily campaign assessments & control maps.
- **NATO** — North Atlantic Treaty Organization: Collective-defense alliance; doctrine source.
- **OCHA** — UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs: Runs ReliefWeb and HDX.
- **PRIO** — Peace Research Institute Oslo: Conflict research & battle-deaths data.
- **RUSI** — Royal United Services Institute: UK defense & security think-tank.
- **SIPRI** — Stockholm International Peace Research Institute: Arms transfers & military-spending data.
- **UCDP** — Uppsala Conflict Data Program: Academic organized-violence dataset (GED).

### ew

- **EA** — Electronic Attack: Jamming/disruption of the EM spectrum.
- **EMCON** — Emission Control: Disciplined control of emissions to limit detection.
- **EMS** — Electromagnetic Spectrum: The range of EM frequencies units must contest.
- **EP** — Electronic Protection: Protecting friendly use of the EM spectrum.
- **ES** — Electronic Support: Searching/intercepting EM emissions for SA/threat warning.
- **EW** — Electronic Warfare: Military use of the EM spectrum (EA/EP/ES).
- **RF** — Radio Frequency: EM emissions used for comms, radar, control links.

### fires

- **counter-battery** — Counter-battery fire: Detecting and suppressing enemy indirect fire.

### geoint

- **chronolocation** — Chronolocation: Estimating time from shadows/sun in imagery.
- **geolocation** — Geolocation: Determining where imagery was captured.

### humanitarian

- **IDP** — Internally Displaced Person: Displaced within their own country.
- **IPC** — Integrated Food Security Phase Classification: Standard for acute food-insecurity/famine phases.

### maritime

- **STS** — Ship-to-Ship transfer: At-sea cargo transfer; sanctions-evasion indicator.

### medical

- **CASEVAC** — Casualty Evacuation: Evacuation of casualties (non-dedicated assets).
- **MARCH** — Massive hemorrhage, Airway, Respiration, Circulation, Hypothermia: TCCC treatment priority sequence.
- **MEDEVAC** — Medical Evacuation: Evacuation on dedicated, marked medical assets.
- **TCCC** — Tactical Combat Casualty Care: Battlefield trauma care guidelines.

### pnt

- **GNSS** — Global Navigation Satellite System: Satellite positioning (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou).
- **GPS** — Global Positioning System: US GNSS constellation; common jam/spoof target.
- **PNT** — Positioning, Navigation, Timing: Services degraded by GNSS denial; need alt-nav.

### security

- **COMSEC** — Communications Security: Protecting communications from exploitation.
- **OPSEC** — Operations Security: Protecting indicators that reveal intentions.
- **PERSEC** — Personal Security: Protecting personal info that enables targeting.

### sensors

- **EO** — Electro-Optical: Visible-band imaging sensors.
- **FLIR** — Forward-Looking Infrared: Thermal imaging sensor for day/night detection.
- **NDVI** — Normalized Difference Vegetation Index: Multispectral index; reveals disturbance/digging.
- **SAR** — Synthetic Aperture Radar: Radar imaging through cloud/dark (e.g. Sentinel-1).

### threat

- **EOD** — Explosive Ordnance Disposal: Render-safe/disposal of explosive hazards.
- **IED** — Improvised Explosive Device: Homemade explosive device; major mobility threat.
- **UXO** — Unexploded Ordnance: Munitions that failed to detonate; hazard to movement.

### tracking

- **ADS-B** — Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast: Aircraft position broadcast; basis of flight trackers.
- **AIS** — Automatic Identification System: Ship position broadcast; basis of vessel trackers.
- **SDR** — Software-Defined Radio: Reconfigurable radio for monitoring RF signals.
- **TLE** — Two-Line Element set: Orbital data used to predict satellite passes.

### uas

- **C-UAS** — Counter-Unmanned Aircraft System: Detect/track/defeat hostile drones (defensive).
- **ELRS** — ExpressLRS: Open long-range RC control-link protocol common on FPV.
- **FPV** — First-Person View (drone): Pilot-controlled drone flown via onboard video.
- **UAS** — Unmanned Aircraft System: Drone + control + comms; the air + ground segments.
- **UAV** — Unmanned Aerial Vehicle: The aircraft element of a UAS.
- **loitering munition** — Loitering munition: One-way attack drone that loiters then strikes.
